Stint Planner

Fuel, tyres, pit stops and driver swaps for endurance races.

Race
Fuel

Leave the start fuel empty for a full tank. The margin is fuel that always stays in the tank.

Tyres

You get new tyres at a stop when the current set would drop below the minimum during the next stint. Wear 0 means never change.

Pit stop

Ticked: the stop takes as long as the slower of the two. Unticked: their times are added up.

Drivers

The starting numbers are placeholders. Use your own numbers from practice. Everything is saved in this browser.
