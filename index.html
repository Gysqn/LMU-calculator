Stint Planner

Fuel, tyres, pit stops and driver swaps for endurance races.

Fuel
Tyres

Drive a few laps in practice and note how much the tyres dropped. You get new tyres at a stop when the current set would go below your lowest condition during the next stint.

Tyres are changed 2 or 4 at a time. With too few tyres to stay above your lowest condition, the changes are spread evenly over the race.

Pit stop

Ticked: the stop takes as long as the slower of the two. Unticked: their times are added up.

Drivers

Drivers take turns from top to bottom. The top one starts the race.

The starting numbers are placeholders. Use your own numbers from practice. Everything is saved in this browser.
