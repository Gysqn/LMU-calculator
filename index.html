Stint Planner

Fuel, tyres, pit stops and driver swaps for endurance races.

Race
Fuel

Leave the start fuel empty to start with a full tank. The margin is fuel that always stays in the tank.

Tyres

Tyres are changed at a stop when the current set would drop below the minimum during the next stint. Set wear to 0 to never change tyres.

Pit stop

Ticked: stationary time is the longer of refuelling and the tyre change. Unticked: they are added up. A driver swap is added when the driver changes.

Drivers

The starting numbers are placeholders. Enter your own lap time, fuel use, tyre wear and pit times from practice.
